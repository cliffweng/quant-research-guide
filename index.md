# Quant Research Guide

A practical study guide for aspiring quant researchers — research workflow and interview prep for QR / research-intern roles, and for self-study. Each topic is a short page, not a textbook chapter.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic in the left nav is a **standalone page you can read in ~10 minutes**.
- Every page follows the same shape: **why it matters**, **core concepts**, a **mental model**, **interview questions** with brief answer keys, and a short **watch list** of verified YouTube videos.
- Topics are ordered as a research workflow: from the question, through data, alpha, and testing, to how you present the work. You can read them in order, or jump straight to the topic you need.
- Nothing here requires a backend, an API key, or a login — it's just reading.
